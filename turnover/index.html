TURNOVER

Time is Money, Tables are Territory.

    New game

    How many servers are on tonight?

    How to play

    You are rival servers on the same floor. Whoever pockets the most tips by close wins.

    Your turn

    • Place the tile you drew on any glowing spot. It must touch the floor already built.
    • Take 3 actions. Tap a table, then use the button: claim an open table, serve its next step, or turn it over once it reaches Check.
    • You can hold 3 tables at once. Service always runs Menu, Drinks, Appetizers, Main Course, Dessert, Check.
    • After everyone has gone, one Event card sets a rule for the whole next round.

    Tips

    • $2 per seat, paid when you turn the table over. Totals round up to the dollar.
    • +50% if the table touches a Window or a Bar when it pays out (it does not stack).
    • Tables more than 2 hexes from the Kitchen are far: Main Course costs 2 actions. A table touching a Server Station is never far.

    Cards

    • Challenge: drawn every time you serve Main Course. Some hurt, some help.
    • Special Request: sometimes attached when you claim. Hit the step in time for a bonus; ignore it for no penalty.
    • Upgrades: spend tips during your turn. Tips spent are gone from your score.

    Closing time

    When the tile stack runs out, the round is finished and the game ends. Tables still mid-service pay nothing.
